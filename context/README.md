# Context

This folder is where the Roast Room learns who you are, so you never explain yourself twice.

Before every council, the Chair reads every `.md` and `.txt` file in here (except this README and `_templates/`). The advisors then judge your decision against your real situation, and the Chair skips questions your notes already answer.

## What to put here

- `about-me.md`: who you are, what you do, who you sell to, your numbers, your limits, how you like to decide. Start from `_templates/about-me.template.md`.
- Any other notes: your offers and pricing, your team, your goals for the year, a strategy doc, past decisions and how they turned out. Plain `.md` or `.txt` files work best.
- `decision-log.md`: the Chair writes this for you. After each council it adds a short entry (the decision, the verdict, the conditions, when to revisit), so the next council remembers.

Keep each file focused and up to date. Out-of-date notes are worse than none. If something changes, edit the file.

## Privacy

Git ignores everything in this folder except this README and the template. Your notes stay on your computer and never go to GitHub, even if your copy of the repo is public. Do not move them out of this folder into files that git tracks.
