# Examples

Three finished councils that ship with the Roast Room, so you can see what a debate and a verdict look like:

- `2026-10-09-example-take-the-seed/`: take a $4M seed round or keep bootstrapping
- `2026-10-10-example-first-sales-hire/`: a first sales hire or keep selling
- `2026-10-10-example-raise-coaching-price/`: raise a coaching price to $3,500

Open the files in order: `brief.md`, `round-1.md`, `round-2.md`, then `verdict.md`.

These are samples about made-up businesses. They are not about you and they did not happen in your context. They are not your past sessions, so the room's **Past sessions** list does not show them, and no council ever reads them as your history. Your own councils are saved in `sessions/`, and what the Roast Room remembers about you lives in `context/`.
